College of Agriculture & Innovation, Iseyin

Iseyin, Oyo State

Floors were treated with industrial scrubbing machines and cleared immediately using automated vacuum mops, leaving every surface ready for handover.

  • Lobby cleaning — 1,560 sq/m
  • Machine floor scrubbing with automated vacuum mopping
