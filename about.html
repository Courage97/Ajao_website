Our Story

Built On Three Decades Of Trust.

Ajao Spotless Cleaning Concept Nig. Ltd has spent over three decades earning its place as one of the first dedicated cleaning and facility-support companies in Nigeria. What began as a commitment to doing the job properly has grown into a nationwide operation trusted by universities, government bodies, and major private organisations.

We are headquartered in Oyo State, with branches in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, and mobile facilities that let our teams travel to a client's site anywhere in the country. Every job is backed by sophisticated cleaning equipment and industrial-grade chemicals, handled by personnel trained to work to a single standard, wherever the job is.
